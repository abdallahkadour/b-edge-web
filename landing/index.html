Privacy policy

This policy explains what B-Edge collects and why. It applies to the B-Edge website and applications.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, phone number and email, so an artist knows who is booked in.
  • Booking information — the services, times, locations and prices of appointments you make.
  • Address information — only where you request a service at your own location, so the artist can reach you.
  • Images — photographs artists upload to their own portfolios.
  • Technical data — basic device and log information needed to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To create, confirm and manage your appointments.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders and changes — including by WhatsApp, where you have provided your number for that purpose.
  • To let artists and salons manage their schedules and earnings.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent misuse.

Sharing

Your details are shared with the artist or salon you book with, because they need them to provide the service. We use service providers for messaging, hosting and image storage, who process data only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data.

Messaging

Where you provide a phone number, we use it to send messages about your own bookings — confirmations, reminders, changes and cancellations. You can stop these at any time by replying STOP or contacting us.

Retention and your rights

We keep booking records for as long as needed to run the service and meet legal obligations. You may request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete your account by contacting us at the address above.

Children

B-Edge is not intended for use by anyone under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will make the updated version available here.

Terms of service

What B-Edge is

B-Edge is a booking platform. Beauty services are provided by the independent artists and salons listed on it, not by B-Edge. Your agreement for the service itself is with that artist or salon.

Bookings and deposits

A booking is confirmed once the artist accepts it. Where a deposit is required, the amount and deadline are shown before you commit. Deposits are arranged directly with the artist.

Cancellations

Cancellation terms are set by each artist or salon and shown at the time of booking. Please cancel as early as you can so the slot can be offered to someone else.

Your account

Keep your account details accurate and your access secure. Do not use B-Edge unlawfully, or to harass artists or other users.

Content

Artists are responsible for the portfolio images and service descriptions they publish, and confirm they hold the rights to them.

Liability

B-Edge is not liable for the quality or outcome of services provided by independent artists and salons. Concerns about a service should be raised with the provider; you can also contact us and we will assist where we can.
