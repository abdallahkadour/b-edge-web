Frequently asked

I don't have a card - how do I pay?

No card needed. B-Edge is billed manually via OMT or Whish, the way you're already used to sending and receiving money in Lebanon. Your dashboard shows exactly what's due and where to send it - once we confirm it's arrived, you're all set for the month.

Does B-Edge take a cut of my bookings?

No. Deposits go straight from your customer to you. Your subscription is the only fee - it doesn't change no matter how many bookings you take that month.

What happens if I have staff artists?

Studio and Multi-Location plans include a few staff seats up front, with a small per-seat fee for each additional artist beyond that - so pricing grows with your team instead of forcing you into a bigger tier all at once.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. If a price ever changes, you keep whatever you signed up at unless you choose to move - we'll never raise what you're currently paying without telling you first.
